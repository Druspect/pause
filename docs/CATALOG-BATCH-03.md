This batch widens Pause beyond the earlier systems shelf. Each candidate must have a primary publisher, documented steward, or durable archival institution; a stable canonical URL; an identifiable original era; and a specific technical reason to preserve it.

## Admission rules for this batch

Candidates that are only summaries, mirror pages without durable provenance, unstable personal copies, or generalized nostalgia collections will remain out of the public shelf. The final batch may be smaller than this list if a canonical primary source or accurate access note cannot be confirmed.
