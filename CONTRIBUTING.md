# Contributing to Pause

Pause is a small editorial archive of links, not an automated feed. Contributions should improve a reader’s ability to locate, understand, and return to an important piece of pre-generative-AI computing knowledge.

## What to submit

Please suggest an original manual, source-code release, paper, software-architecture explanation, language reference, system document, video with technical context, or durable archive. The best entries were created before generative AI became routine in programming practice, remain legally and practically accessible, and explain a meaningful step in computing history.

## Required information

Every proposed source should include the source title and URL, the original publication or creation year where available, the subject area, and two or three sentences explaining why a future builder should care. Please identify access restrictions, copyright uncertainty, dead links, or known archival mirrors.

## Editorial standard

Pause prefers primary sources and durable institutions over unsourced summaries. It does not accept commercial link placement, duplicated collections without added context, or sources whose principal value is nostalgia rather than technical understanding.

When you are uncertain, open an issue with the `source-proposal` label and explain the case. A good contribution should help preserve a chain of human reasoning, not simply add another URL.
